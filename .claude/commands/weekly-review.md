---
description: The Monday review of the network, written from three commands - what needs a decision, how every site is trading, and the agreements and year end dates coming up.
---

1. Run, `--json` each: `node scripts/franchise.mjs attention`, `node scripts/franchise.mjs network`, `node scripts/franchise.mjs renewals`.
2. Write four short sections, prose plus small tables, nothing invented:
   - **Today's decisions.** The attention list, worst first, one action each.
   - **Trading.** Sites up and down on the quarter, arrears by site, reports missing. Each currency on its own.
   - **Field.** Visits overdue, critical and major findings open, the lowest visit scores.
   - **Agreements and the Code.** Notices due in the next 90 days, cooling off periods running, year end deadlines.
3. End with at most five actions for the week, each one a single command or phone call.
4. On paper: `npm run view` renders the week and royalties pages in the franchisor's brand.
