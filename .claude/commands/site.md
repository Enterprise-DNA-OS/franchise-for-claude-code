---
description: One site's whole card before any call or visit - the franchisee, insurance, agreements and notice dates, six months of royalties, visits and open findings, tickets, certificates, breach notices and notes.
---

1. Run `node scripts/franchise.mjs site "<name, ref or franchisee>" --json`. If it lists several matches, ask which one.
2. Open with anything that would embarrass the operator on the call: overdue royalties, an open breach notice, lapsed insurance, an expired certificate, a critical finding, an agreement past its notice date.
3. Then the card in short sections: who and where, the agreement (term, notice due by, notice given), royalties last six months, visits and findings, tickets, certificates, notes.
4. End with the three things worth raising on the call, each tied to a record.
